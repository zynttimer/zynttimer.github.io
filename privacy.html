Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the Zynt app (“the app”) for iPhone and Apple Watch uses. The app is developed by Anthony Stepennov.

In short

Zynt does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we run no servers that your data could be sent to.

Information stored on your device

Your timer lists and settings (such as language, sounds, haptics and notifications), and whether Zynt Pro is unlocked, are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud Sync (part of Zynt Pro), your timer lists are also stored in your own iCloud account so that they appear on your other devices. This data is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time in the app’s settings.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice control (part of Zynt Pro) uses the microphone and speech recognition only after you turn it on, and only while the app is open. What you say is used solely to recognize commands such as “start” or “pause”; it is not recorded, stored or sent to us. Where your iPhone supports it, recognition happens entirely on the device; otherwise it is processed by Apple’s speech recognition service under Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can revoke these permissions at any time in the iPhone’s Settings.

Siri

If you use Siri phrases such as “Zynt, listen”, your request is handled by Siri under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The app only receives the resulting command.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app shows and controls the timer running on your iPhone. The two exchange timer information directly with each other; nothing is sent to us.

Notifications and Live Activities

Notifications and the Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island are created on your device. No push service or server is involved.

Purchases

Zynt Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details or your Apple ID; Apple only confirms to the app that the purchase was made.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to us at anton.stepennov@gmail.com.
