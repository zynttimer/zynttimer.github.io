Support

Need help with Zynt? Write to us at anton.stepennov@gmail.com — please mention your iPhone (and Apple Watch) model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

“Settings” below means the gear icon on Zynt’s main screen.

How do I control timers by voice?

Voice control is part of Zynt Pro. Tap the microphone at the top of the main screen and say “start”, “pause”, “continue”, “next” or “stop”. While a timer’s name is being announced, the microphone pauses for a moment.

How do I open Zynt hands-free?

Tell Siri “Zynt, listen” or “Listen with Zynt” — Zynt opens with the microphone already on. You can also say “Start Zynt”, “Pause Zynt”, “Resume Zynt”, “Next in Zynt” or “Stop Zynt”. To have the microphone turn on after any Siri command, keep Settings → Listen After Siri on. (“Open Zynt” may simply open the app without listening.)

How do I use the Apple Watch app?

The Apple Watch app is part of Zynt Pro. Start a timer on your iPhone, then open Zynt on your watch to see the countdown and to stop, pause or skip timers. If Zynt isn’t on your watch yet, install it from the Watch app on your iPhone.

How do I show the timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island?

Turn on Settings → Live Activity in Zynt (part of Zynt Pro). If nothing appears, check that Live Activities are allowed for Zynt in the iPhone’s Settings.

How do I restore Zynt Pro on a new device?

Open Settings → Zynt Pro in the app and tap “Restore Purchases”, signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase.

How do I sync my lists between devices?

Turn on Settings → iCloud Sync (part of Zynt Pro) on each device, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I repeat a list several times?

Touch and hold “Start Timer” and choose the number of laps.

Notifications don’t appear

Make sure Settings → Notifications is on in Zynt and that notifications are allowed for Zynt in the iPhone’s Settings.

Can I see the introduction again?

Yes: Settings → How to Use Zynt.

How do I change the app’s language?

Settings → Language. “System Default” follows your iPhone’s language.
