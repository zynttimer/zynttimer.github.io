Zynt

Timers that run back to back

Build a list of timers once — warm-up, sets, rests, cool-down — and Zynt runs them one after another, with sounds, spoken timer names and laps. For workouts, Pomodoro, cooking, studying or any routine. With Zynt Pro: unlimited lists, Live Activities, voice control, Siri, iCloud sync and an Apple Watch app.
